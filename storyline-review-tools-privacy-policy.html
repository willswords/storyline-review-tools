Privacy Policy for Storyline Review Tools

Storyline Review Tools is a browser extension designed to assist with reviewing and testing Articulate Storyline courses in Articulate Review.

Data accessed

The extension accesses information from Articulate Storyline courses opened by the user in Articulate Review. This may include course configuration, slide data, navigation settings, and quiz/question information. This information is used only to provide the extension's course-review and testing features.

Local storage

The extension uses browser storage to remember the user's selected extension options and preferences.

Data transmission and sharing

Storyline Review Tools does not transmit, sell, share, or disclose collected course information or user information to the developer or to third parties. Course information is processed locally in the user's browser.

External services

The extension operates on Articulate Review course content. It does not use external analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-collection services.

Data retention

The extension does not maintain a remote database or server for storing user or course information. Extension preferences may remain in the browser's extension storage until they are changed, cleared, or the extension is removed.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change.
